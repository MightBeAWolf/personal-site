---
title: Kubernetes
summary: A container orchestration platform for deploying, scaling, and self-healing distributed workloads.
link: https://kubernetes.io/
---

Kubernetes schedules containers across a cluster of machines, restarts
failed workloads, and gives services a consistent way to discover and load
balance one another — the standard substrate for running distributed
systems at any real scale.

Per the résumé, this covers greenfield Kubernetes buildouts and
high-availability clusters engineered to bridge legacy tooling with modern
containerized workflows — RAG-backed AI agents included. For smaller,
single-operator deployments, <button type="button" class="glossary-term" data-glossary-term="k3s">K3s</button> is the lighter-weight version of the same idea.
